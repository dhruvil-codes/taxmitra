Section 142(1) is titled inquiry before assessment. For assessment purposes,
the Assessing Officer may require a taxpayer to produce, or cause to be
produced, accounts or documents that the officer requires. A scrutiny workflow
should therefore treat requested books, financial statements, and bank records
as evidence requests, not as optional background material. The response should
be organized request-by-request and should avoid inventing facts that are not
supported by taxpayer records.
