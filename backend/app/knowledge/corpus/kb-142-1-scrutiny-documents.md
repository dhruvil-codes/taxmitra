Section 142(1) is titled inquiry before assessment. For assessment purposes,
the Assessing Officer may require a taxpayer to produce, or cause to be
produced, accounts or documents that the officer requires. For assessment years
beginning before April 1, 2026, this provision is governed by the
Income-tax Act, 1961. A scrutiny workflow should therefore treat requested
books, financial statements, and bank records as evidence requests, not as
optional background material. The response should be organized request-by-request
and should avoid inventing facts that are not supported by taxpayer records.
