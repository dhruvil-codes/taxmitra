Section 142(1) allows the Assessing Officer to require written and verified
information on points or matters needed for assessment. For a scrutiny notice,
requests such as sources of cash deposits or explanations for significant
credits and debits should be answered with record-backed written explanations.
If the taxpayer is unsure, the safer path is verification or professional
review before taking a final position.
