Section 142(1) allows the Assessing Officer to require written and verified
information on points or matters needed for assessment. For assessment years
beginning before April 1, 2026, this provision is governed by the
Income-tax Act, 1961. For a scrutiny notice, requests such as sources of cash
deposits or explanations for significant credits and debits should be answered
with record-backed written explanations. If the taxpayer is unsure, the safer
path is verification or professional review before taking a final position.
