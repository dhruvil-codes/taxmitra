Section 144 deals with best judgment assessment. One trigger is failure to
comply with all terms of a notice issued under Section 142(1). A Tax Mitra
scrutiny workflow should therefore surface missing evidence and uncertainty
clearly, recommend review where needed, and never present an incomplete package
as safely ready for submission.
