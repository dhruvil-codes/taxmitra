Sometimes information is reported against the wrong person: a shared or mis-typed PAN, a family member's account, or a reporting error by the payer. In that case the taxpayer should disagree, stating that the amount was never received and is not attributable to them. The strongest evidence is the bank statement for the relevant period showing no such credit, together with Form 26AS/AIS to reveal exactly what was reported and by whom. If the entry belongs to a relative, the response should say so plainly.
