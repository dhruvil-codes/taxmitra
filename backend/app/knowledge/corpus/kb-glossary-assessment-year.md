Income tax in India is discussed in two linked periods. The Financial Year (FY) is the year in which income is earned, running 1 April to 31 March. The Assessment Year (AY) is the year immediately after, in which that income is assessed and taxed — for example, income of FY 2024-25 is assessed in AY 2025-26. Notices typically reference the Assessment Year, so a mismatch notice for "AY 2025-26" concerns income earned during 2024-25.
