Straightforward information mismatches can usually be responded to by the taxpayer directly. Other situations carry higher stakes and shorter, stricter timelines — for example reassessment notices under section 148, notices requiring detailed inquiries under section 142(1), or prosecution-adjacent matters. For those, and for any notice a taxpayer does not fully understand after reading a plain-language explanation, consulting a chartered accountant or an authorised tax practitioner before responding is the prudent course. Tax Mitra deliberately refuses to guide citizens through notice types where the risk of a wrong step is high.
