An intimation under section 143(1) communicates the CPC processing result for a filed return. Tax Mitra should compare the return and the processed figures shown in the intimation, identify any stated difference, and explain whether the result is a refund, demand, or no further action. If there is a mistake apparent from the record, the official e-Filing portal provides a CPC rectification route for an intimation under section 143(1). The Department also provides a Tax Credit Mismatch route for TDS, TCS and challan details. The portal record and the taxpayer's documents control whether either route is appropriate.

This final intimation is not the same as a notice under section 143(1)(a): 143(1)(a) is the proposed-adjustment response path, while 143(1) is the completed processing result. Tax Mitra must not generate a response to a proposed adjustment from this knowledge item.
