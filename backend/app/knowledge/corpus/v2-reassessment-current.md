The official reassessment FAQ states that after 1 April 2026, proceedings for earlier Assessment Years through AY 2026-27 can continue under the transition rules and may refer to the Income-tax Act, 1961, including approval provisions for sections 148 and 148A. Because the legal route depends on the notice, dates, jurisdiction and facts, Tax Mitra should explain identified facts and route the taxpayer to qualified professional review rather than prepare an automated legal response.
