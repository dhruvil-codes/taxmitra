Banks, financial institutions, and other specified entities are required to report prescribed financial transactions to the Income Tax Department under section 285BA. These reports — popularly visible to taxpayers through Form 26AS and the Annual Information Statement (AIS) — include payments such as interest, dividends, mutual fund redemptions, and high-value transactions. The Department matches this reported information against each taxpayer's return. When reported income does not appear in the return, the difference can surface as a mismatch intimation. Taxpayers can view exactly what was reported about them by downloading Form 26AS and the AIS from the e-Filing portal, which is the recommended first step when investigating any mismatch.
