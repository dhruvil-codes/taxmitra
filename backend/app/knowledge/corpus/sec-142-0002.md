**142(2)** For obtaining full information in respect of the income or loss of any person, the Assessing Officer may make such inquiry as he considers necessary. **142(2A)** The official text covers a directed audit where, having regard to the nature and complexity of the accounts and the interests of the revenue, the Assessing Officer considers an audit necessary and obtains the prescribed previous approval. **142(2C)** The official text addresses the period for furnishing the audit report and permits extension for good and sufficient reason, subject to an aggregate limit of 180 days from receipt of the direction under sub-section (2A). **142(3)** The assessee shall, except where the assessment is made under section 144, be given an opportunity of being heard in respect of material gathered on the basis of an inquiry under sub-section (2) or an audit under sub-section (2A) and proposed to be used for the assessment. ## Curation and verification note This local capture was taken from the official CBDT page on 27-Aug-2026.
