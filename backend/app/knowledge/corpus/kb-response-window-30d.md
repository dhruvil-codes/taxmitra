Mismatch intimations and e-campaign communications typically specify a response window — commonly 30 days from the issue of the intimation — within which the taxpayer should respond online. If no response is filed within the window, the proposed adjustment may be treated as accepted and any resulting demand becomes payable. The exact window for a specific notice is stated in the notice itself and must always be read from the notice rather than assumed. Tax Mitra computes the deadline from the notice's issue date and the category's standard window, and always shows the number of days remaining so the taxpayer can act in time.
