When the income in question was already included in the return, the appropriate response is to disagree with the proposed adjustment and demonstrate where the income appears in the return. Useful attachments are: the relevant pages or computation sheet of the filed return showing the income head and amount, and Form 26AS/AIS so the Department can see the matching entry. The response should identify the head of income under which the amount was returned. This situation commonly arises when interest is auto-added under "Income from other sources" but the AIS entry is compared against a different line, or when the same income is reported with minor differences of rounding.
