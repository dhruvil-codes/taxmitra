When a taxpayer files a return of income, the Income Tax Department processes it and sends an *intimation* under section 143(1). The intimation compares the return as filed with the Department's computation and informs the taxpayer of any adjustments. An intimation is a communication, not a demand by itself: it tells the taxpayer what the Department's records show, what was adjusted, and what the taxpayer can do about it. Adjustments under section 143(1) include corrections of arithmetical errors, disallowance of certain losses, and additions of income found in information statements that does not match the return. The taxpayer can agree with an adjustment, or respond online disagreeing and providing an explanation.
