The Compliance Portal is available through e-Filing single sign-on for Annual Information Statement, e-Campaign, e-Verification, e-Proceedings and DIN Authentication. The portal's active campaign or e-Verification record determines the action available. AIS is an information view with taxpayer feedback; an AIS entry is not by itself a finding that income is taxable. Tax Mitra may explain the record and help organize verification, but must not convert an AIS entry or campaign into an unsupported tax conclusion.
