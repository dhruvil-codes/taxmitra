When the income in question was genuinely received but not included in the return, the honest response is to accept the inclusion. The taxpayer indicates agreement, the amount is added to total income, and the Department recomputes the tax along with applicable interest. Accepting a genuine omission promptly is routine and avoids harsher consequences of unexplained mismatch later. Useful attachments are the payer's interest certificate or Form 16A, a computation of total income, and the bank statement showing the credit.
