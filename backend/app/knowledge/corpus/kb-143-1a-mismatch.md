Clause (a) of section 143(1) allows the Department, while processing a return, to add back income that appears in the taxpayer's information statements (such as Form 26AS, AIS, or SFT reports) but is not included in the return of income. This is the typical cause of an "income mismatch" intimation: the Department's records show receipts — for example, interest paid by a bank — that the return does not reflect. The adjustment increases total income by the unmatched amount and computes tax accordingly. The taxpayer is given an opportunity to respond online before the adjustment is finalised. A mismatch does not automatically mean the income is taxable to the taxpayer: the information may be wrong, may relate to another person's account, or the income may already be included in the return under a different head.
