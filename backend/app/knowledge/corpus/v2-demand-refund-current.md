The official portal provides a response route for outstanding demand and an adjustment-against-refund communication under section 245. If the taxpayer does not respond to an outstanding-demand communication, the demand may be confirmed or adjusted against a refund according to the portal notice. Refund reissue is a separate registered-user service for a refund that failed; it requires a filed return, a validated bank account, and portal verification. Tax Mitra can explain the record and checklist, but must not infer that a demand is payable or draft a disputed legal response without grounded facts.
