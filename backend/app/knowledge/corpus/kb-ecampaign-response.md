Responses to mismatch intimations and e-campaign communications are submitted online on the official e-Filing portal, under the "Pending Actions" workflow. The taxpayer selects the relevant communication, reviews the information the Department holds, chooses whether the income relates to them and whether it was reported, writes a response (or accepts a suggested one), attaches supporting documents, and submits. The portal generates an acknowledgement. A response can also be submitted through the e-Proceedings facility where applicable. Tax Mitra does not submit anything itself — it helps the taxpayer prepare the response, which the taxpayer then submits on the official portal. The official portal remains the only authoritative channel for submission.
