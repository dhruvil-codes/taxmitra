The current Income Tax Department e-Proceeding FAQ distinguishes proceedings under the Income-tax Act, 1961 from proceedings under the Income Tax Act, 2025. For a Tax Year 2026-27 onward proceeding that identifies the new Act, use the 2025 Act context. For an earlier Assessment Year, do not silently substitute new-Act guidance. The notice and portal record remain the controlling sources for the exact section, deadline, and response path.
