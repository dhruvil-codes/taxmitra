The official e-Filing route for notices and intimations is Dashboard > Pending Actions > e-Proceedings. The portal lists defective-return notices under section 139(9), prima facie adjustments under section 143(1)(a), rectification under section 154, and notices or clarification communications from an Assessing Officer or other Income Tax authority. The taxpayer or authorised representative views the notice, chooses Submit Response, supplies only the information requested by that proceeding, reviews the response, and completes the portal's required declaration or verification. Tax Mitra may prepare guidance and a reviewable action plan; it must not submit on the taxpayer's behalf.
For a 139(9) response, the official manual describes Agree or Disagree. Agree uses the applicable ITR type and corrected JSON where the portal asks for it; Disagree requires the taxpayer's reason. The notice itself controls the deadline and requested material.
