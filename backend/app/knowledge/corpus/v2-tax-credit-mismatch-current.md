The Income Tax Department's Tax Credit Mismatch service covers mismatches involving TDS, TCS, advance tax, regular tax and related credit records. A taxpayer may need to compare the return with Form 26AS and the portal record. Depending on the mismatch, correction may involve the deductor or collector filing a correction statement, or a taxpayer route such as rectification or a revised return where the official rules allow it. A rectification request is for a mistake apparent from the record; it is not a route to add new income or a new deduction. Tax Mitra must show the mismatch and official route without deciding disputed facts.
