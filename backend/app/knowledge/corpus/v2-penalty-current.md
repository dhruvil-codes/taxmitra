Penalty-related communications may be available through e-Proceedings, but the applicable provision, facts, limitation, evidence and defence are notice-specific. Tax Mitra may identify the communication, preserve deadlines and organize the taxpayer's documents. It must not generate a legal defence or assert that a penalty is payable without authoritative, case-specific grounding and professional review.
