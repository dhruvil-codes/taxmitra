An intimation is the standard communication the Income Tax Department sends after processing a return. It shows the returned income, the Department's computed income, and the difference, if any. Receiving an intimation does not mean the taxpayer has done something wrong — it means the Department's records and the return do not agree, and the taxpayer is being told so and given a chance to respond.
