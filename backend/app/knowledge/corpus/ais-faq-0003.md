FAQs on AIS (Annual Information Statement) **Q-1 What is Annual Information Statement (AIS)?** Annual Information Statement (AIS) is comprehensive view of information for a taxpayer. The objectives of AIS are: • Displays complete information to the taxpayer before filing the return of income with a facility to capture online feedback • Promotes voluntary compliance and enable seamless prefilling of return • Deters non-compliance
